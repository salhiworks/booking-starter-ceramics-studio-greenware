## Photographs — Pexels License

Fourteen frames from fourteen Pexels photographs by one photographer, Yan Krukau, from one shoot
in one pottery workshop, downloaded into the project (never hot-linked), then cropped, graded once
for the whole set and exported for their frames. Only the processed frames are in this
repository. They are sample imagery: nobody shown works at, studies at or endorses the
(fictional) studio, none of the pictured pottery is its students' work, and the page labels every
photo "Sample" and says the photographs show another pottery workshop. Full details and crops:
`PHOTOS.md`.
